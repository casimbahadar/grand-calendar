Beginner Line Dancing

One Session (October 19 - December 14)

Monday Evenings • 6:30–8:00 PM
New to line dancing? No problem!
The first 30 minutes (6:30–7:00 PM) is just for new dancers—learn the basic steps in a relaxed, supportive setting.
Then stick around for the full beginner-level class from 7:00–8:00 PM, where you'll dance to a fun mix of upbeat music—not just country! From classic favorites to pop and easy-listening hits, this class is all about great music and great energy.
What to bring:
  • Comfortable shoes that slide easily (avoid grippy soles)
  • A water bottle
  • A positive attitude and a willingness to have fun!
Whether you're dancing solo or with friends, this class is perfect for anyone looking to move, laugh, and learn something new.

Refunds & Transfers Policy

Program and Workshop Transfers & Refunds

We will gladly issue a refund for 100% of the cost of the activity charged to you provided the activity withdrawal request is received up to and including 5 BUSINESS DAYS PRIOR to the activity start date. 

If after attending the first class you decide a program is not right for you, we are happy to issue a pro-rated refund. This request must be made at least a day prior to the start of the second class. Refunds will be subject to an administrative fee of 10% to up to $25.00.  If you wish to leave funds on your account, there will be no administrative fee and credit will stay on file for 36 months.

Missed classes, outside of facility closures due to weather or instructor cancellations, will NOT be refunded or credited to account. 

Refunds or credits will not be issued for withdrawal after the second class. Special circumstances may be considered by the Director of Community Services and may require supporting documentation.
