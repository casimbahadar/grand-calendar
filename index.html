    About and privacy

    About Grand Calendar

    A free app that gathers events, programs and activity providers across Waterloo Region and Stratford in one place. No ads, no accounts, nothing to pay for.

    Where the information comes from

    Events: Explore Waterloo Region and Waterloo Public Library. Programs: the City of Waterloo program guide and the Township of Wellesley's programs page. Providers: each provider's own website, checked by hand and rechecked monthly. Every item links to its source.

    Ages, times and prices are shown only as each source states them. Details change, so check with the source before you go or register.

    Venue locations © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Text set in Atkinson Hyperlegible (SIL Open Font License).

    Privacy

    Everything you save, your settings and your location stay on this phone. Grand Calendar has no accounts and collects nothing about you.

    To show events, this phone reads Explore Waterloo's public calendar directly, and looks up new venue addresses on OpenStreetMap (the address only, never your location). Your location, if you share it, is used only on this phone, rounded to about 1 km.

    Removing the app from your Home Screen deletes everything saved in it. Use Calendar, List, Export saved to keep a backup.
